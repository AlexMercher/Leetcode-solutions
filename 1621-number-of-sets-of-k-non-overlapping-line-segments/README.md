## Explanation

DP is calculating exactly these values.

For example, when we're at i = 4:

dp[0] = 1
dp[1] = 4
dp[2] = 6
dp[3] = 4
dp[4] = 1

These are:

4C0 = 1
4C1 = 4
4C2 = 6
4C3 = 4
4C4 = 1

Then when i = 5, we calculate:

5C2 = 4C2 + 4C1
    = 6 + 4
    = 10

That's exactly what:

dp[2] = dp[2] + dp[1];

is doing.

So the whole DP is basically building Pascal's triangle:

             1
           1   1
         1   2   1
       1   3   3   1
     1   4   6   4   1
   1   5  10  10   5   1
