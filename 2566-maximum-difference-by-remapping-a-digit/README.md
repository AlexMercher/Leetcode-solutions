## Explanation

For maximum:

Find first digit != 9
↓
change every occurrence of it to 9

Why first? Because the leftmost changed digit has the highest place value.

For minimum:

Find first digit != 0
↓
change every occurrence of it to 0

Again, we want to minimize the most significant digit possible.

For:

11891

maximum:

first non-9 = 1
11891
↓
99899

minimum:

first non-0 = 1
11891
↓
00890

which as an integer is 890.
