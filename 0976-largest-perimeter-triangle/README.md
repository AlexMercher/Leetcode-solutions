## Explanation

If the two smaller sides together are longer than the largest side, the other two inequalities are automatically true.

For example:

1, 2, 2

1 + 2 > 2 
1 + 2 > 2 
2 + 2 > 1 

So 1,2,2 forms a triangle.

But:

1, 2, 3

1 + 2 > 3
3 > 3 ❌

It's exactly equal, so the points lie on a straight line. The area is zero.

Why do we scan from the largest side?

We want the largest perimeter: a+b+c 

After sorting:

1 2 2 3 4 5

The largest possible perimeter would ideally use:

3,4,5

So start from the largest three.

If they form a triangle:

3 + 4 > 5 ✅

then that's immediately the largest perimeter, because any other triple must replace one of these sides with something smaller.

If they don't:

3 + 4 <= 5

then we cannot use 5 with 3 and 4.

Could some other pair with 5 work?

No. 3 and 4 are already the two largest sides available before 5, so any other pair would have sum even smaller.

Therefore we discard 5 and try:

2,3,4

This gives a very clean greedy strategy.
