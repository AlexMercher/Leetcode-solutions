## Explanation

For:

        3
       / \
      9  20
         / \
        15  7

At 3:

left child = 9
9 has no children

So 9 is a left leaf:

answer = 9 + sum(right subtree)

Go to 20:

left child = 15
15 has no children

So:

answer = 15 + sum(right subtree)

7 is a right leaf, so it isn't counted.

Final:

9 + 15 = 24
The mental model

Whenever a tree problem asks about a special kind of node, ask:

What relationship makes this node special?

Here, "left leaf" is not just a property of the node itself.

It's:

parent
  |
  L  ← must be parent's LEFT child

and L must also have:

L->left  == nullptr
L->right == nullptr
