## Explanation

The trick is to avoid calculating the bits of every number independently.

The transition

Look at consecutive numbers:

0  → 0 ones
1  → 1 one
2  → 1 one
3  → 2 ones
4  → 1 one
5  → 2 ones
6  → 2 ones
7  → 3 ones
8  → 1 one
...

Notice:

i = even

means its binary representation is just the representation of i/2 with a 0 appended:

6 = 110
3 = 11


110 = 11 + 0

Therefore:

ans[i] = ans[i/2]

For odd i:

i = 2k + 1

its binary representation is the representation of k with a 1 appended:

5 = 101
2 = 10


101 = 10 + 1

Therefore:

ans[i] = ans[i/2] + 1

We can combine both into:

ans[i] = ans[i >> 1] + (i & 1);
