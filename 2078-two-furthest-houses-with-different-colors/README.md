## Explanation

Pair (1,2) is valid, but distance 1.

The farther pairs involving the ends beat it:

0 ↔ 1 : 1 vs colors 1,2
0 ↔ 2 : 2 vs colors 1,2

The middle positions can participate, but they never give us a distance better than what an endpoint can provide.

That's why checking the two ends is sufficient.

When maximizing j - i, pushing i left or j right can only increase the distance. So if the color constraint prevents one endpoint from being pushed outward, examine the other endpoint.
