## Explanation

Why reverse the DFS?
Normally, water flows from a cell to a neighboring cell whose height is less than or equal to its own.
For example:
5 → 3 → 1 → Ocean


Instead of asking, "Can this cell reach the ocean?", we ask:
Starting at the ocean, which cells can reach it?

We reverse the traversal. Starting from a cell of height 3, we can visit a neighboring cell of height 5, because water could flow from 5 down to 3.
So the reverse DFS condition is:
heights[nr][nc] >= heights[r][c]


Approach
Maintain two visited matrices:
- pacific[r][c]: can reach the Pacific?
- atlantic[r][c]: can reach the Atlantic?
Run DFS from the Pacific borders (top and left), then from the Atlantic borders (bottom and right).
Finally, cells where both matrices are true belong in the answer.
