## Explanation

Case 1: One type dominates

Suppose:

amount = [10,2,2]

The other two types together give:

2 + 2 = 4

So after pairing those 4 cups with 4 of the 10 cups, we still have:

6 cups

of the same type.

Those 6 require 6 seconds individually.

Total:

4 + 6 = 10

This is actually just: maximum

when the largest amount is greater than the other two combined.

Why? Every second can fill at most one cup of that dominant type.

Case 2: No type dominates

For example:

[5,4,4]

Total cups:

13

Every second can fill at most 2 cups, so we need at least:

13/2 = 7 

seconds.

And because no type dominates, we can keep pairing different types enough to achieve that bound.

So the answer is:

max(mx,⌈sum/2⌉)
