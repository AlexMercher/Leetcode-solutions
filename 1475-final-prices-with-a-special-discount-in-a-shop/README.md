## Explanation

Suppose:

8 4 6 2 3

Stack stores indices whose discount hasn't been found yet.

At 4:

8 > 4

So 4 is the first valid discount for 8:

8 → 8 - 4 = 4

At 6:

8 is already handled

At 2:

6 >= 2

so:

6 → 6 - 2 = 4

and then:

4 >= 2

so:

4 → 4 - 2 = 2

At 3:

2 < 3

so nothing is resolved.

Final:

[4,2,4,2,3]
Complexity:

Each index is pushed once, popped at most once therefore time: O(n), space:O(n)
