## Explanation

Dry run
nums = [1,2,3,4]
First pass: prefix

prefix means:

product of everything before i

Initially:

prefix = 1
ans = [1,1,1,1]

i = 0:

ans[0] = 1
prefix = 1 * 1 = 1
ans = [1,1,1,1]

i = 1:

ans[1] = 1
prefix = 1 * 2 = 2

i = 2:

ans[2] = 2
prefix = 2 * 3 = 6

i = 3:

ans[3] = 6
prefix = 6 * 4 = 24

Now:

ans = [1,1,2,6]

This currently represents:

index 0 → product before 1 = 1
index 1 → product before 2 = 1
index 2 → product before 3 = 1*2 = 2
index 3 → product before 4 = 1*2*3 = 6
Second pass: suffix

Now suffix means:

product of everything after i

Start:

suffix = 1

i = 3:

ans[3] *= 1

so:

ans[3] = 6

Then:

suffix = 1 * 4 = 4

i = 2:

ans[2] *= 4
ans[2] = 2 * 4 = 8

Then:

suffix = 4 * 3 = 12

i = 1:

ans[1] *= 12
ans[1] = 1 * 12 = 12

Then:

suffix = 12 * 2 = 24

i = 0:

ans[0] *= 24
ans[0] = 24

Final:

[24,12,8,6]
Why zeros work automatically

Take:

nums = [-1,1,0,-3,3]

For the zero at index 2, the prefix is:

-1 * 1 = -1

and suffix:

-3 * 3 = -9

So:

answer[2] = (-1) * (-9) = 9

For every other position, one side contains the zero, so their answer becomes 0.

Final:

[0,0,9,0,0]

No special zero handling is needed.
