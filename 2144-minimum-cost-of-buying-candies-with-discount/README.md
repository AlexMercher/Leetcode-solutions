## Explanation

### Why the greedy is safe

After sorting:

largest
second largest
cheapest

For every group of 3, making the cheapest one free is always optimal.

You cannot make the expensive one free because the free candy must satisfy:

free <= minimum(bought two)

So in a sorted triple:

a >= b >= c

the only sensible free candy is c.

That is exactly why your descending traversal works.

Time: O(n log n)
Extra space: O(1) apart from the sorting implementation.
