## Explanation

After sorting:

highest units/box
        ↓
next highest
        ↓
...
lowest units/box

A box can use exactly one slot on the truck. Therefore, every slot should be filled with the box having the highest available units.

For example:

[5,10]
[2,5]
[4,7]
[3,9]
truckSize = 10

Sort:

[5,10]
[3,9]
[4,7]
[2,5]

Take:

5 boxes × 10 = 50
3 boxes × 9  = 27
2 boxes × 7  = 14

Total:

50 + 27 + 14 = 91
