## Explanation

Dry run
[1,5,2,4,1]

5 > 1 → nothing

2 <= 5:

adj = 5 - 2 + 1 = 4
2 → 6
cost = 4

Now:

[1,5,6,4,1]

4 <= 6:

adj = 6 - 4 + 1 = 3
4 → 7
cost = 7

Now:

[1,5,6,7,1]

1 <= 7:

adj = 7 - 1 + 1 = 7
1 → 8
cost = 14

Final:

[1,5,6,7,8]

Answer:

14

The general rule is:

When nums[i] <= nums[i-1], increase nums[i] to exactly nums[i-1] + 1, so the required operations are nums[i-1] - nums[i] + 1.
