## Explanation

Digits:

0 0 4 9

We can make:

4
9

and the two zeroes can be used as leading zeroes:

04 → 4
09 → 9

So:

4 + 9 = 13

This means the general solution is:

Sort the four digits.
Take the two smallest digits as the leading digits of the two numbers.
Append the remaining two digits appropriately.

In fact, after sorting, the answer is still:

10(a+b)+c+d 

where:

a <= b <= c <= d

Why?

We form:

10a + c
10b + d

and add:

10a+c+10b+d =10(a+b)+c+d

For 4009:

a=0, b=0, c=4, d=9

10(0+0)+4+9 = 13

which corresponds to:

04 + 09 = 4 + 9 = 13
