## Explanation

e use Dynamic Programming where dp[i] represents the minimum cost required to reach step i. We initialize dp[0] = 0 because the starting position has no cost. For every step i, we consider three possible ways to reach it: jumping from i-1, i-2, or i-3. The corresponding jump costs are 1, 4, and 9. Additionally, every jump includes cost[i-1], the cost of landing on the current step. We take the minimum among these three possibilities. Finally, dp[n] gives the minimum cost to reach the destination.

TC: O(n)
SC: O(n)
