## Explanation

They can only win if its not a multiple of 4. Eg Till 3 stones you can take all 3,but at 4 you lose no matter what.

At 5,6 & 7 you can take 1,2 or 3 stones respectively and make the opponent lose.

---

## My Notes

This can also be solved with boolean DP where the DP is dp[i]=!(dp[i-1] && dp[i-2] && dp[i-3]) because if all 3 states of dp are true for your opponent then you lose otherwise you can win in the given dp[i] state.

Extra space=O(n). Thus not preferred here.
